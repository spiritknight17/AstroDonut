We collect the following types of information to provide you with a smooth and personalized experience:

AstroDonut uses your data to:

3. How We Protect Your Information


We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure your personal data is secure, including:

However, please note that no method of online transmission or storage is 100% secure.

AstroDonut does not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only with:


• Service Providers: Such as payment processors, delivery partners, and IT support, strictly for order fulfillment.


• Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights, users, or operations.


5. Your Rights


Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to:


• Access, correct, or update your personal data.


• Withdraw consent to data processing.


• Request deletion of your data, where applicable.

You may contact us anytime to exercise these rights.
